# MomentTune ArtBox Product Book

## Project Vision

MomentTune ArtBox is an Interactive Wellness Content Platform that provides emotional experiences based on the user's current state.

It is not a place to display interactive art. It is a platform that turns emotion into sensory experience through visuals, sound, motion, rhythm, and interaction.

## Mission

Help users recognize, regulate, and gently shift their emotional state through short, beautiful, interactive content experiences.

## Core Value

- Emotional Awareness: help users notice their current state without judgment.
- Interactive Healing: use touch, motion, sound, and rhythm as emotional regulation tools.
- Personal Resonance: make content feel relevant to the user's moment.
- Gentle Technology: keep technology supportive, not dominant.
- Daily Wellness: fit emotional care into short everyday moments.

## Why This Project Exists

Most digital content is designed to capture attention. ArtBox is designed to return attention to the user.

Users often know they feel anxious, tired, scattered, or low, but do not always know what to do next. ArtBox provides a low-friction emotional reset: choose a state, enter an interactive session, and leave a little more grounded.

## Long-Term Vision: 3 Years

### Year 1: Emotional Content Lab

- Define emotional content categories.
- Build a metadata-driven content engine.
- Launch Calm, Focus, Breathing, Sleep, Creativity, Nature, Meditation, Drawing, Music Reactive, and Generative Art prototypes.
- Add session feedback and basic recommendation metadata.

### Year 2: Personalized Wellness Experience

- Connect ArtBox with MomentTune user state data.
- Personalize content by mood, time, weather, stress, sleep, HR, and HRV.
- Learn from completion, interaction, and before/after feedback.

### Year 3: Wellness Content Platform

- Open a creator-ready content format.
- Support branded wellness, education, and guided emotional programs.
- Build AI-assisted personalization and adaptive content parameters.

## Design Philosophy

- Emotion first, interface second.
- Calm by default.
- Minimal UI during immersion.
- Beautiful interaction over decorative effects.
- Accessibility and performance are part of the emotional experience.

## Emotional Experience

ArtBox experiences are designed around emotional shifts:

- Anxious to Calm
- Distracted to Focused
- Tired to Restored
- Low Energy to Energized
- Lonely to Comforted
- Blocked to Creative
